What Aetheris does

Four jobs, one public utility

Shade the planet

A thin sunshade far out between the Sun and Earth dims sunlight by about one percent. A council of nations decides how much.

How the sunshade is built →

Re-seed burned land

Gliders drop from the edge of space and scatter seed capsules over burned forests, then glide home on sunlight.

How the gliders fly →

Clean the oceans

Ships twice the size of aircraft carriers sweep plastic out of the ocean and turn it into parts for reef-repair drones.

Shared by everyone

No country owns it. An open accord, free public data and launches sold at cost keep it accountable to all.
